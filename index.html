IdeaStone

Learning Intelligence Platform

Make high-quality learning adaptive, interactive, and accessible.

IdeaStone is an in-development learning intelligence product with both commercial and public-benefit goals. Its vision is to compile knowledge into high-density, interactive learning experiences that can adapt to the learner instead of delivering the same static material to everyone.

01

Learning intelligence

Combine learner modeling, personalized routing, adaptive scaffolding, AI tutoring, simulations, and reusable interactive modules into one coherent learning system.

02

Knowledge compilation

Turn source knowledge into carefully structured courses and multiple learning representations rather than treating course generation as a one-shot text task.

03

Public benefit

A core part of the vision is widening access: keeping high-quality public learning resources affordable and creating support paths for students who would otherwise be priced out of advanced AI-assisted learning.
